WHO WE ARE

Every person deserves the opportunity to thrive.

Foundation for Africa Empowerment (FAE) is a Community Based Organization established to support positive and sustainable transformation in Sierra Leone.

We work to uplift vulnerable and disadvantaged groups, especially women, children, young girls and youths. Through education, health, economic opportunities, peacebuilding and cultural development, FAE seeks to help individuals and communities build a stronger future.

Established29 January 2014
Registration typeCommunity Based Organization (CBO)
Operational areaSierra Leone, West Africa

◎OUR VISION

A future where everyone can reach their full potential.

An empowered, healthy, peaceful and self-reliant Africa where women, children, young girls and youths reach their full potential and live in dignity.

↗OUR MISSION

Turning opportunity into lasting community change.

To empower women, children, young girls and youths in Sierra Leone through quality education, health care, economic opportunities, peace building, and cultural development for sustainable community transformation.

OUR COMMITMENT IN ACTION

Our objectives

FAE works across connected areas of development to create opportunities, strengthen communities and support lasting change.

01▤

Education

Improve access to quality formal and non-formal education, literacy, mentorship and scholarships for children, girls and youths.

02✚

Health

Promote community health awareness, maternal and child health, mental health support and access to basic health services.

03♡

Drug Abuse Prevention

Prevent and reduce substance misuse among youths through awareness, counselling, rehabilitation referral and peer education.

04☮

Peace & Non-Violence

Promote peace, conflict resolution, dialogue and non-violence in schools and communities.

05↗

Economic Empowerment

Support women and youths with vocational skills, entrepreneurship training, financial literacy and small business support.

06♧

Agriculture

Promote modern farming, food security, climate-smart agriculture and youth involvement in agribusiness.

07♫

Music, Art & Culture

Use music, art and culture to promote talent, healing, identity, peace and community development.

08⚽

Sports

Promote sports for discipline, health, teamwork, talent development and positive youth engagement.

09♀

Gender Equality

Promote gender equality, girls' education, and protection of women and girls against gender-based violence and discrimination.

10≋

Water & Sanitation

Improve access to clean drinking water, hygiene and sanitation facilities in schools and communities.
